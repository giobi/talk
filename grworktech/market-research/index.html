01Executive Summary

GR Work Tech deploys persistent AI workspaces for knowledge-intensive companies, starting with HR/Recruitment. The market is massive — €45–50B global AI agents and knowledge management (TAM), €5–7B in European knowledge-intensive business services (SAM), with a realistic €1–3M capture over 3 years through direct network (SOM) — growing fast at 10–14% CAGR, and fragmented across point solutions. No competitor combines persistent memory, agentic capability, custom deployment, and LLM-agnosticism. The gap is structural: every existing tool resets to zero between sessions. Brain Protocol fills that gap. Value compounds over time, creating a moat that deepens with every client engagement, every methodology captured, and every insight retained.

03The Structural Gap

What No Competitor Does

No existing product combines persistent institutional memory, methodology capture, cross-client intelligence, and knowledge that compounds over time. Every tool on the market resets to zero between sessions. Recruitment firms rebuild context manually, lose expertise when consultants leave, and cannot leverage patterns across placements.

Brain Protocol is the only product that becomes more valuable the longer you use it. Each placement, each candidate interaction, each market insight is retained, structured, and available to the entire team — permanently.
