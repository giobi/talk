### What is Brain Protocol?

An open specification for building AI agent workspaces with persistent memory. Every company gets its own "brain" — a structured, AI-readable knowledge base that grows over time and enables autonomous action.

Unlike generic AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot), Brain Protocol doesn't forget. Unlike traditional knowledge management, it acts — sending emails, producing deliverables, managing workflows.

### The Memory Model: WESP

| Layer | Maps to | What it stores |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **W**orking | Context window | Current session, active conversation |
| **E**pisodic | diary/ | What happened, when, why — chronological record |
| **S**emantic | wiki/ | People, companies, projects, processes — permanent knowledge |
| **P**rocedural | skills/ | How to do things — automations, integrations, playbooks |

Inspired by cognitive neuroscience (Tulving 1972, Baddeley & Hitch 1974). Markdown-first, git-versioned, LLM-agnostic.
