The Problem

  • AI tools are stateless — they forget everything between sessions
  • Institutional knowledge is fragile — it lives in people's heads, not in systems
  • When people leave, expertise walks out the door
  • Generic AI — ChatGPT, Copilot — can't capture what makes YOUR company unique

The problem isn't AI capability. It's AI memory.

02

The Solution

Brain Protocol — an open specification for AI agent workspaces with persistent memory.

Architecture Structured knowledge base wiki, diary, skills — organized by memory type, not by file format
Memory Model WESP Framework Working, Episodic, Semantic, Procedural — mirrors how humans retain knowledge
Infrastructure LLM-agnostic, git-versioned Markdown-first, auditable history, no vendor lock-in
Core Promise The AI learns your business And never forgets. Session to session, year to year.
